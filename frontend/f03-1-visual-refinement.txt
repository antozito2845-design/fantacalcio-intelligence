F03.1 — VISUAL REFINEMENT & PREMIUM THEME POLISH
Date: 2026-08-22

WHAT IMPROVED
- Dark canvas now uses a deeper midnight foundation, restrained dual radial
  atmosphere, stronger surface separation and more deliberate border light.
- Light mode has its own bright neutral canvas, high-contrast typography,
  explicit sidebar treatment and softly layered white surfaces.
- Sidebar branding, active state, hover behavior and lower foundation card now
  read as a cohesive navigation rail in both themes.
- Sticky topbar has clearer glass layering, refined search/profile controls and
  stronger separation from dashboard content.
- KPI cards add domain-relevant icon tiles, controlled accent color, improved
  number hierarchy and richer but restrained highlights.
- Auction panel gains a compact role-needs summary. Formation gains field
  markings and an explicit chemistry label. Matchup adds non-text-only recent
  form marks. Scouting rows and the table gain borders, hierarchy and hover
  polish.
- Role, confidence, difficulty, trend, status and price primitives retain
  semantic meaning while gaining clearer physical presence.
- Spacing, title weight, header dividers and panel density were aligned into a
  more consistent commercial dashboard rhythm.

REFERENCE SELF-CHECK
The result is materially closer to the supplied premium sport-tech reference
through its midnight navy depth, cyan/blue accents, refined rail and topbar,
compact KPI row, sculpted panels, analytical pitch and richer tabular surface.
It deliberately does not copy player identities, club branding, photography or
real business data. The foundation remains a reusable static preview rather
than implying completed backend features.

LIGHT MODE SELF-CHECK
Light mode is now intentionally designed instead of being a token inversion:
the rail is clearly separated, text remains strong, card boundaries remain
visible, the canvas has subtle depth and all colored signals keep readable
contrast.

RESPONSIVE SELF-CHECK
Browser validation at 1440, 1280, 1024, 768 and 390 px found no global
horizontal overflow. The sidebar transitions from full to compact to hidden;
dashboard grids reflow to two and one columns; internal table scrolling remains
contained.
